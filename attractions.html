🏨 Pulitzer Amsterdam

A collection of 25 connected canal houses and your base in the heart of the Nine Streets.

Visitor tipFamily-friendly planning

Don’t miss

  • Historic canal-house interiors
  • Inner garden
  • Jansz., Pulitzer’s Bar and the hotel boat
Kerby Family Tip: Your room is never far away—use the hotel as a natural rest stop between the Jordaan and the city centre.

📚 Anne Frank House

A deeply moving museum centred on the Secret Annex and Anne Frank’s life and writing.

Visitor tipFamily-friendly planning

Don’t miss

  • The Secret Annex
  • Anne’s original diary and writings
  • Otto Frank’s reflections
Kerby Family Tip: Allow about 60–90 minutes. Expect steep stairs and no photography inside. Tickets are timed.

🎨 Rijksmuseum

The Netherlands’ national museum, presenting Dutch art and history from the Middle Ages onward.

Visitor tipFamily-friendly planning

Don’t miss

  • The Night Watch
  • The Milkmaid
  • Gallery of Honour
  • Cuypers Library
Kerby Family Tip: Allow 2–3 hours. Start with the Gallery of Honour, then choose a few rooms rather than trying to see everything.

🌻 Van Gogh Museum

The world’s largest collection of works by Vincent van Gogh, arranged around his life and development.

Visitor tipFamily-friendly planning

Don’t miss

  • Sunflowers
  • Almond Blossom
  • The Bedroom
  • Self-portraits and letters
Kerby Family Tip: Allow 90–120 minutes. Timed entry is essential; the museum shop is particularly good.

🚤 Pulitzer’s Boat

A classic canal cruise departing from the hotel’s own dock aboard a beautifully restored salon boat.

Visitor tipFamily-friendly planning

Don’t miss

  • Canal-ring architecture
  • A relaxed view of the city from the water
  • Small-scale hotel departure
Kerby Family Tip: Arrive at the hotel dock about 15 minutes early and bring a light layer even in summer.

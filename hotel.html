Pulitzer Amsterdam

Your canal-house home base, with hotel dining, boat links and nearby orientation.

Pulitzer Amsterdam is made up of 25 linked 17th- and 18th-century canal houses between Prinsengracht and Keizersgracht—an ideal base for the Jordaan and Nine Streets.

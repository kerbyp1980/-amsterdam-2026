The Nine Streets

A curated neighborhood guide to coffee, shops, treats and the canals just outside the Pulitzer.

The Nine Streets—De 9 Straatjes—cross the main canal ring immediately beside the Pulitzer. This is your neighborhood guide for browsing, coffee, gifts and an unhurried canal-side wander.

Best approach: Do not try to “complete” every street. Pick two or three shops, stop for coffee, and enjoy the bridges and canal views between them.

A Relaxed 60–90 Minute Walk

Start at the Pulitzer

Exit toward Reestraat and begin with the hotel’s canal-house frontage.

Reestraat & Hartenstraat

Browse PLUK and the small fashion, gift and design shops; pause on the bridge for a canal view.

Wolvenstraat & Berenstraat

Continue across Keizersgracht, looking back along the canal before exploring the middle streets.

Runstraat

Stop at Screaming Beans or browse Skins, then cross back toward the Prinsengracht.

Return through the canals

Loop back to the Pulitzer, or continue north into the Jordaan and Winkel 43 if everyone still has energy.

Kerby Family Tip: The area is at its best when you leave space for spontaneous stops. Shops are small and opening hours can vary, so check the linked official pages on the day.

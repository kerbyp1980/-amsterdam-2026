Practical Information

The small details that make the days easier and keep personal information private.

Emergency

112 for police, ambulance or fire in an emergency. For non-urgent help, ask the Pulitzer concierge for the most appropriate local service.

Getting Around

Most of your central itinerary is walkable. Use contactless payment on Amsterdam public transport where accepted, and always check in and out.

GVB official site

Money & Tipping

The Netherlands uses the euro. Cards are widely accepted. Service is included; rounding up or leaving a modest tip for excellent service is customary rather than compulsory.

Cycling Awareness

Bike lanes are active traffic lanes. Look both ways before crossing and do not stand in them while checking directions.

Weather

August can shift quickly between sun, cloud and showers. Carry a compact rain layer and check a current forecast each morning.

Dutch weather service

Tickets & Privacy

Keep museum tickets, booking references, passports and confirmations in the private family folder—not on this public GitHub Pages site.

Useful Dutch: Hallo = hello · Dank u wel = thank you · Alstublieft = please/you’re welcome · Spreekt u Engels? = Do you speak English?
